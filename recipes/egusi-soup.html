Egusi Soup

Home

Description

A native Nigerian soup primarily known to the Yorubas (south-west region).

Ingredient

  • Grounded melon(egusi)
  • Blended Pepper & Tomato mix
  • Maggi cubes & Salt
  • Palmoil
  • Sliced onions
  • Iru (Locust beans)
  • Sliced Ugwu (vegetable)
  • Protein (Fish, Ponmo, Assorted Meat)

Steps

  1. In a small bowl, mix the grounded egusi with little water to form a loose paste
  2. Pre-boil the assorted meat until tender. Save the meat stock
  3. Pour Palm oil into a pot on medium heat and allow it to warm up (but do not bleach), add the sliced onions, iru and egusi paste to the oil to fry.
  4. Stir in the blended pepper mix
  5. Then, pour the reserved meat stock, also add the protein, maggi and salt to taste, then allow it to cook for 10-15 minutes.
  6. Wash the sliced ugwu, and add it to the egusi and allow it to simmer on low heat for 3-5 minutes until the vegetables soften, then turn off the heat.
