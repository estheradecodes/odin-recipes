Fried Rice

Home

Description

A vibrant party-style west-african rich dish cooked in seasoned meat stock and stir-fried with curry spices, sweet corn and chopped meat & green vegetable.

Ingredient

  • Washed Rice
  • Stock (chicken/beef)
  • Seasoning (curry powder, thyme, seasoning cubes)
  • Chopped onions
  • Groundnut (cooking) oil
  • Protein (cooked beef/chicken)
  • Fried rice seasoning powder
  • Salt
  • Vegetables (finely diced; carrots, green beans, green peas, sweetcorn, green ball pepeer and spring onions)

Steps

  1. Season and boil the protein(chicken) with onions, curry, thyme and seasoning cubes until tender, fry or grill the chicken and save that rich chicken stock
  2. Heat oil into the main pot, add in the chopped onions and stir-in fried rice seasoning powder(i.e SpiceCity or Larsor)
  3. Then, add the washed raw rice directly into the pot and stir fry unti the rice are well-coated and fried into its beautiful signature green colour
  4. Afterwards, pour in chicken stock (or water if you don't have stock, just slightly above the level of the rice), add curry powder and salt to taste, stir thoroughly and cover the pot, allow it to cook until rice is soft and liquid is fully absorbed
  5. Saute the veggies; in a separate frying pan, heat a little oil and lightly stir-fry the diced vegetables until they're cooked and crunchy
  6. Finally, Pour the stir-fried vegetables into the pot of cooked rice, mix everything together thoroughly until they are evenly distributed

    7. Remember to serve it hot alongside your fried chicken!
