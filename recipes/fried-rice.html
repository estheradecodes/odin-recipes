Fried Rice

Home

Description

A beloved west-african dish known for its aromatic scent and taste.

Ingredient

  • Washed Rice
  • Grounded Pepper & Tomatoes mix
  • Sachet tomato paste
  • (Sliced) Onions
  • Groundnut oil
  • Bay leaves
  • Maggi cubes & Seasoning
  • Salt
  • Meat stock

Steps

  1. Pour in the Groundnut oil into the pot on the heat(gas/stove)
  2. Then the sliced onions and tomato paste go in
  3. Afterwards, pour in the grounded pepper&tomato mix, let it cook/fry for 3-5 minutes, make sure you add in maggi cubes and seasoning with salt to taste.
  4. Add in washed rice and then, meat stock, stir thoroughly and add not-too-much water(sits just slightly above the level of the rice), cover the pot tightly and cook on low-medium heat until the liquid is fully absorbed and the rice is tender
